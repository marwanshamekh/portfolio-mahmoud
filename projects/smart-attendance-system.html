Smart Attendance System

An AI attendance system that detects and recognizes faces in real time and manages attendance automatically. It won 2nd place in Ramadan Camp 2026 (AI & Computer Vision).

Python PyQt5 OpenCV YOLOv8 DeepFace OpenFace

2nd place, Ramadan Camp 2026

The project placed second in the AI & Computer Vision track of Ramadan Camp 2026. The award ceremony took place during the “Research X” event, hosted by the IEEE BUB RAS Chapter (Robotics & Automation Society) of the IEEE BUB Student Branch.
